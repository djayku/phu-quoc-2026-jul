⚠️ Honest Flags on the Itinerary

  • VinWonder removed. It's a full-day park on its own — combining it with Safari in one day with 5 kids aged 5–10 is too much. Safari alone from Movenpick is the right call.
  • Safari moved to Day 6 (Movenpick stay). It's 30–40 min from Movenpick vs. 70+ min from Sun Tropical. Animals are technically more active in the morning but afternoon Safari is still excellent.
  • 9–10am departures throughout. No one is eating breakfast at 7:30am on holiday with 5 kids. All activities re-timed around realistic departures.
  • Island hopping removed. July = rougher seas, windier conditions. The boat tours to distant islands (Mong Tay, Hon Dua) are not ideal this time of year. The cable car + Aquatopia is a better use of the day.

Ganh Dau — Crystal Reef Snorkeling

🌊 Snorkeling · North Phu Quoc · Best for families
★★★★★ · #1 family snorkel spot in Phu Quoc ✓ Operating
📍 Ganh Dau, North Phu Quoc · ~30–40 min from Movenpick
Best snorkeling for your group. Calm, clear, shallow coral reefs — perfect for kids aged 5–10. Vibrant coral gardens, sea fans, tropical fish. The north coast is sheltered by the island, so July's rougher seas don't affect it as much. Included as a morning activity on Day 6 before Safari. Bring reef-safe sunscreen and snorkel masks (or rent on site for ~100k VND/person). Book a speedboat snorkel tour via OnBird or Pelago for the full experience with a guide, or DIY at Ganh Dau beach with rented gear. 📍 Open in Maps

Ong Lang Beach — Shore Snorkeling + Paddleboard

🤿 Snorkeling · Paddleboard · South-west coast
Walkable from Movenpick · Free to enter beach ✓ Operating · Low-key activity
📍 Ong Lang Beach · 4 min walk from Movenpick
Low-key option for Movenpick mornings (Days 5 & 7). Snorkel straight from the shore — decent coral near the rocks at either end of the beach. Paddleboards sometimes available from beachfront stalls. Water is calm in the mornings before the afternoon wind picks up. Bring your own snorkel masks or buy a cheap set in Duong Dong town. Gear rental ~100k VND/person. 📍 Open in Maps

Honest Take on July Conditions

North coast (Ganh Dau + Cua Can): Sheltered by the island — calmer water than the south. Best snorkel spot in July.
Hon Thom Island (Aquatopia day): The beach there has calmer water than Bai Kem — worth a snorkel if you have time.
Skip distant island tours (Mong Tay, Hon Dua): July's wind and rougher seas make the 45–60 min boat rides unpleasant, and snorkel quality drops with choppy water.
Gear: Bring snorkel masks if you have them. Rent on-site for ~100k VND/person/day. Kids will need properly fitted masks — buy in HK or at WinMart+ on island.

Practical Tips

🚗 Transport

  • Ms. Ngoan — [contact on request] · WhatsApp · Deliver to the villa
  • Scooter: ~25,000–40,000 VND/day. Car hire also available.
  • Grab app — like Uber. For 9 people you'll need 2 Grabs.
  • Pre-book airport transfers via Klook to lock in price.

🧳 Pack for July

  • Rain ponchos × 6 — July downpours come fast
  • Reef-safe SPF50+ sunscreen (buy in HK — expensive in Phu Quoc)
  • Quick-dry clothes (NOT cotton for activities)
  • Mosquito repellent (deet-free for kids)
  • Light long-sleeve — cable car is breezy + AC
  • Water shoes — for Aquatopia and rocky areas
  • Power bank — long days out drain phones
  • Reusable water bottles × 5

💰 Money

  • VND — USD 1 ≈ VND 25,000 · HKD 1 ≈ 3,000 VND
  • Cards accepted at resorts/restaurants
  • Cash for markets, street food, tips
  • Budget: ~15–20M VND cash for 9 people over 8 days
