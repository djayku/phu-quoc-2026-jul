⚠️ Honest Flags
What Changed & Why

VinWonder Removed — Safari Kept

  • VinWonder is a full-day park on its own. Combining it with Safari in one day with 5 kids aged 5–10 is chaos. Safari alone from Movenpick is the right call.

Safari Moved to Day 6 — From Movenpick

  • It's 30–40 min from Movenpick vs. 70+ min from Sun Tropical. Same experience, smarter logistics. Animals are technically more active in morning but afternoon Safari is still excellent.

9–10am Departures Throughout

  • No one is eating breakfast at 7:30am on holiday with 5 kids. All activities re-timed realistically.

July Sea Conditions — Skip Distant Island Tours

  • July means windier seas. Skip Mong Tay and Hon Dua snorkel tours — 45–60 min boat rides are rough. Stick to the north coast (Ganh Dau) and Cua Can River instead.

Family Notes
Practical Tips

🚗 Transport

  • Ms. Ngoan — +84 908 954 195 · WhatsApp · Deliver to P909
  • Scooter: ~25–40k VND/day. Car hire also available.
  • Grab app — like Uber. Need 2 Grabs for 9 people.
  • Pre-book airport transfers via Klook to lock price.

🧳 Pack for July

  • Rain ponchos × 6 — July downpours come fast
  • Reef-safe SPF50+ sunscreen (buy in HK — expensive in Phu Quoc)
  • Quick-dry clothes (NOT cotton for water activities)
  • Mosquito repellent (deet-free for kids)
  • Light long-sleeve — cable car is breezy + AC
  • Water shoes — Aquatopia + rocky snorkel spots
  • Power bank — long days out drain phones
  • Reusable water bottles × 5
  • Snorkel masks if you have them (kids sizes)

💰 Money

  • VND — USD 1 ≈ VND 25,000 · HKD 1 ≈ 3,000 VND
  • Cards accepted at resorts/restaurants
  • Cash for markets, street food, tips
  • Budget: ~15–20M VND cash for 9 people over 8 days

🏥 Health & Safety

  • Phu Quoc General Hospital: +84 297 386 1111
  • International Clinic: +84 297 626 9898
  • Police: 113 · Fire: 114 · Ambulance: 115
  • Movenpick: +84 297 269 9999
  • Buy travel insurance before departure

🌦️ July Weather Plan

  • Mornings (7–11am): Beach, Safari, snorkel, kayak
  • Midday (11am–3pm): Pool, villa, AC restaurants
  • Afternoons: Shows, sunset, markets
  • Rain backup: Grand World mall + cinema
